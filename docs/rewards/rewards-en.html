Test phase

The YEET token is in its test phase on the BNB Smart Chain testnet. Testnet tokens have no value and cannot be traded. It has not been decided when the token will be available on the main network, and there is no entitlement to it.

4. Daily limit and pool protection

Daily limit

Each account is credited at most 1,000 points in rewards per calendar day (UTC). Once the limit is reached you can keep using Yeet, but further actions earn no points that day. If a reward would exceed the limit, only the remainder up to the limit is credited. Nothing carries over to the next day.

The conversion pool

Points can only be converted to YEET while the part of the token supply reserved for this still holds YEET. That reserve is 15.75 billion YEET (75 % of the total supply) and technically cannot be exceeded. Its current state is public: justyeet.it/api/v1/tokens/pool.

Reduced rewards when the pool runs low

If the remaining pool falls below 10 %, rewards are halved and rounded down to whole points:

5. Using points

Tips

You can give points to other users as a tip – on a post, in a direct message or during a live stream.

  • Minimum amount: 0.01 points.
  • The recipient receives 90 %; 10 % goes to Yeet as the platform fee.
  • You cannot tip yourself, and tips are not possible between accounts that have blocked each other.
  • A tip is final and cannot be taken back.

Pay-per-view

When publishing a post, the author can set a price in points. Others unlock the post for that price.

  • Same split as tips: 90 % to the author, 10 % platform fee.
  • Once unlocked, always unlocked; you are never charged twice.
  • Before buying, you confirm that you want the digital content immediately and that your right of withdrawal ends as a result (Terms of Service, section 6).

Paper wallets

A paper wallet is a voucher for points that you create and then print or pass on as a code. Whoever redeems the code receives the points.

  • You can issue one once your e-mail address is confirmed. Maximum per paper wallet: 1,000,000 points.
  • The points are deducted from your balance and set aside when you issue it. No fee – the full amount arrives.
  • You can void a paper wallet that has not been redeemed yet; the points then return to you. You cannot redeem your own paper wallet.
  • Like cash: whoever holds the code can redeem it. Keep it safe accordingly.

Live promotion

Where the live feature is enabled, hosts can promote their stream:

6. The platform fee

Yeet charges 10 % on tips and pay-per-view purchases. The fee is withheld from the amount sent; the sender pays nothing on top. There is no fee on rewards, paper wallets or the conversion to YEET.

The fees collected are booked in points to a platform account and logged for every single transaction. They can arithmetically replenish the conversion pool – but never beyond the fixed token reserve of 15.75 billion YEET.

7. Converting points to YEET

Requirements

  1. Your own wallet is linked. You connect an external wallet to your account and confirm it with a signature. Yeet does not hold tokens for you; payouts always go straight to your wallet.
  2. Age verification completed.
  3. At least 100 points, in whole points.
  4. Sanctions screening. The destination address must not be on a sanctions list.
  5. Pool available. The conversion pool must cover the amount.

How it works

1 · RequestPoints are deducted at once.
2 · ReviewYeet reviews the request by hand.
3 · ReleaseThe request waits for the next batch run.
4 · PayoutYEET are minted to your wallet.
  • Rate: currently 1 point = 1 YEET. The rate at the moment of your request applies; it is stored with the request.
  • Changes to the rate apply to future requests only and are announced at least 14 days in advance. Current and announced rates: justyeet.it/api/v1/points/rate.
  • Batch runs take place hourly. The token contract additionally limits how many YEET may be minted per day in total; once that limit is reached, the remaining requests wait. No processing time is guaranteed.
  • Costs: Yeet charges no fee and pays the network costs of the payout.
  • Refusal: if a request is refused or cannot be completed, the points return to your balance.
  • Final: a completed conversion cannot be reversed. There is no entitlement to a payment in money.

10. Transparency and protection against abuse

Publicly visible

  • /api/v1/tokens/params – all reward rules with their current values
  • /api/v1/tokens/pool – state of the conversion pool
  • /api/v1/points/rate – conversion rate, including announced changes
  • /api/v1/swap/status – state of the NOTE swap

Safeguards

  • Every movement of points is recorded in an immutable, hash-chained journal.
  • Conversions are reviewed by hand before anything is paid out.
  • Sanctions screening at the request and again at payout.
  • Daily limit, minimum length and "once per post" against collecting without a real contribution.

12. Frequently asked questions

Can I buy or sell points?

No. Points only arise from activity and can only be passed on through the platform's features (tips, pay-per-view, paper wallets).

Do points expire?

There is currently no expiry. Changes to the rules are announced with reasonable notice.

What happens to my points if I delete a post?

Rewards already credited are not reversed when you delete a post. Publishing posts only to collect points and deleting them again breaches the Terms of Service and may lead to a suspension.

Why did I get no points for my post?

The most common reasons: the text was shorter than 120 characters, the daily limit had been reached, or it was not your first comment on that post.

Why do I need age verification to convert, but not to collect points?

Points stay on the platform. Conversion creates a freely transferable token – for that we must make sure an entitled adult stands behind every payout.

How long does a conversion take?

That depends on the review and on demand. Batch runs take place hourly; we do not promise a fixed time.

Can I still get the welcome bonus later?

Yes, as long as some of the first 100,000 places are left – as soon as e-mail confirmation and age verification are complete.
